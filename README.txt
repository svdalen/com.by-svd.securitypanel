Security Panel turns the sensors you already have into a complete alarm system. Door, window and motion sensors arm and disarm in Away and Home mode, with an exit delay to leave the house and an entry delay to disarm when you come home. Every household member gets their own code, and a silent panic code quietly warns someone else while the alarm appears to disarm normally. A keypad widget on your dashboard shows the status and takes codes, a sensor status widget shows which doors and windows are open and when a sensor last tripped, and the Ring Keypad pairs directly with the app so its lights, countdown and siren follow the alarm.

The app connects to the devices in your home to watch your sensors and to drive sirens and indicator lights, which is why it asks for access to the Homey API. Open the app settings to choose delays, which sensors count in which mode, users and codes, sirens, LED lights and which events appear on your timeline.
